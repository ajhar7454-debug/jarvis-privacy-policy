# Privacy Policy — JARVIS

**Last updated:** 17 October 2026

JARVIS (“the App”) is an Android voice-controlled assistant designed to help users interact with their devices through voice commands.

## Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, the App may access:

- **Microphone:** To listen for voice commands and process speech.
- **Accessibility data:** If you enable the Accessibility Service, the App may inspect visible screen content to perform requested actions and assist with screen interaction.
- **Contacts and phone features:** If you grant the required permissions, the App may use contact information to help place calls or perform related commands.
- **Device settings:** To carry out supported commands such as controlling the flashlight, volume, or brightness.

## How Information Is Used

Information accessed by the App is used to provide the features you request, process commands, and improve the reliability and safety of device interactions.

Some features may send voice-command text or screen information to external services when those features are enabled. The information handled depends on the feature and configuration you use.

## Sharing and Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party services to provide certain features. Those services may process information according to their own privacy policies. Information is not sold by the developer for advertising purposes.

## Permissions and Your Choices

You can manage or revoke app permissions through Android settings. Disabling permissions or the Accessibility Service may prevent related features from working.

## Data Security and Retention

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by the App. Data retention depends on the feature and the services involved. This policy should be updated to describe any actual storage, logging, or deletion practices before public release.

## Children's Privacy

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact the developer.

## Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the App's features change. The latest version will be published at this URL.

## Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact the app developer through the contact details provided on the Google Play listing.
